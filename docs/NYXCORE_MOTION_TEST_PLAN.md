# NYXCORE motion verification

After preview deployment:
1. Open `/nyxcore-safe.html` on iPhone/iPad.
2. Confirm visible organisms change position continuously for at least 60 seconds.
3. Background/foreground the page once, then confirm motion resumes.
4. Cycle evolution speed through multiple presets and confirm motion continues.
5. Allow thermal protection to throttle; confirm the engine remains running and organisms still move, even if more slowly.
6. Confirm IndexedDB population is preserved and no reset occurs.
